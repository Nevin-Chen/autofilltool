# My writing voice

Copy this file to `bridge/voice-spec.md` and replace the bracketed parts with
your own writing. The bridge appends this file to the system prompt on every
draft, so it is the single strongest lever for making cover letters and answers
sound like you wrote them, not like a chatbot did.

Two things carry the voice: a short description of how you write, and a few real
samples the model can pattern-match against. The samples matter more than the
description. Paste in your actual writing, unedited.

---

## How I write

Describe your own patterns. Replace these with yours; delete what doesn't apply.

- Sentence length: [e.g. mostly short, one idea per sentence; occasional longer one]
- Tone: [e.g. direct and plain, a little dry, not salesy]
- Openings: [e.g. I start with the concrete thing I did, not "I am excited to apply"]
- Closings: [e.g. I stop when the point is made; no "I look forward to hearing from you"]
- Vocabulary: [e.g. plain words, I say "built" not "spearheaded", "problem" not "challenge"]
- Words I never use: [e.g. leverage, passionate, robust, synergy, delve, seamless]
- Punctuation: [e.g. no em dashes; commas and periods; parentheses for asides]

## Rules

- Write in the first person, as me.
- Match the patterns and vocabulary in the samples below more than any generic
  "good writing" instinct.
- Keep every specific claim grounded in the facts provided about me. Do not
  invent employers, titles, dates, or metrics.
